# KPI Definitions

Every KPI below is defined against the warehouse in `sql/schema.sql`. All of
them exclude cancelled orders (`orders.is_cancelled = 0`) unless the KPI says
otherwise — see `analytics_guidelines.md`.

## Total Revenue

The sum of `order_items.revenue` over the selected transactions.

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(oi.revenue), 2) AS total_revenue
FROM order_items oi
JOIN orders o ON o.invoice_no = oi.invoice_no
WHERE o.is_cancelled = 0;
```

## Order Count

The number of distinct completed invoices.

```sql
SELECT COUNT(*) AS n_orders FROM orders WHERE is_cancelled = 0;
```

## Average Order Value (AOV)

Total Revenue divided by Order Count. Revenue lives on the line item and the
invoice lives on the header, so AOV always needs a join and a
`COUNT(DISTINCT invoice_no)` — counting rows instead of invoices is the most
common way to get this KPI wrong.

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(oi.revenue) / COUNT(DISTINCT o.invoice_no), 2) AS average_order_value
FROM order_items oi
JOIN orders o ON o.invoice_no = oi.invoice_no
WHERE o.is_cancelled = 0;
```

## Average Basket Size

Total units sold divided by Order Count: `SUM(oi.quantity) / COUNT(DISTINCT o.invoice_no)`.

## Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

The total revenue a customer has generated inside the dataset window. This is a
historical CLV, not a predicted one — this project does not model future spend,
only future repeat purchase.

```sql
SELECT o.customer_id, ROUND(SUM(oi.revenue), 2) AS lifetime_value
FROM order_items oi
JOIN orders o ON o.invoice_no = oi.invoice_no
WHERE o.is_cancelled = 0
GROUP BY o.customer_id;
```

## Repeat Purchase Rate

The share of customers with more than one completed order:
`customers with >1 invoice / all customers`. Reported as a percentage. This is
the base rate the repeat-purchase model is compared against.

## First-Order Churn Rate

`1 - Repeat Purchase Rate`. The share of customers who never ordered again
inside the window.

## Revenue Concentration (top-decile share)

The share of total revenue generated by high-value customers (top 10% by
lifetime revenue). A high value means revenue depends on few customers and
retention risk is concentrated.

## Monthly Revenue Trend

Total Revenue grouped by `strftime('%Y-%m', o.invoice_date)`. The first and
last months of the window are partial and must be flagged.

## Cancellation Rate

`cancelled invoices / all invoices`. The one KPI that deliberately includes
`is_cancelled = 1`.

## Units per Product

`SUM(oi.quantity)` grouped by `stock_code`, used for best-seller rankings. Rank
by units for demand questions and by revenue for value questions — they give
different top tens.
